How to take part · How POLY_PHONY works

Each POLY_PHONY work opens its making and revises through your feedback. What you leave becomes the credits.

  1. 01Readthe work and its process
  2. 02Leave a notereference · direction · design feedback / or just sign the guestbook
  3. 03Enter the creditsif adopted: a revenue share in points + credit · even if not, base points and your name in the credits
  4. 04Join the crewone checkbox in the feedback form applies to make the work

Every participant receives, on request, a participation & credit statement, usable as supporting evidence of artistic activity.
