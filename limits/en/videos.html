Feedback

Reference suggestions, directing and design feedback are all welcome. You can attach a reference image (≤5MB) or a link. Attached images and links are private by default and shown on the site only if you allow it. Email is always private.

Feedback requires a login. So we can verify who left it and credit you accurately — with credit and revenue-share points — if it's adopted. Login is just that identity check.

* Private by default to protect personal IP. Shown on the site only if you choose to publish.

Crew recruitment happens at production time. This is currently a low-budget, self-funded project applying to grants; if you signal interest, we’ll email the exact terms and a contract before production (you re-confirm then). Even if you don’t sign on, if your submitted materials are used in the final work you receive points like any contributor — and can choose how you’d like your revenue share.

Adopted feedback accrues as points. For an actual cash payout, choose ‘Production investment’. → Rewards & points
