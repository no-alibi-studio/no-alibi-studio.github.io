Contribution Rewards · Points

how adopted contributions come back to you

Adopted feedback is recorded as a revenue share, and that share is returned to you as site points.

  • Story contributions → e-book revenue share
  • Image / video contributions → screening & exhibition revenue share

Points can be used for the e-book, screenings, exhibitions and goods — and for future works by no-alibi.com. As long as a work earns royalties, points keep accruing.

How points are granted

Points are granted alongside each revenue report.

  • Base points — to every participant, once per piece of feedback, graded by contribution
  • Share-percentage points — to contributors whose feedback is adopted into the final work, at each revenue report

※ Share-percentage points (revenue share) apply when a creative / semantic contribution — to the work's content, story, images or direction — is reflected in the final work. Corrections such as spelling or script formatting aren't eligible for a share, but are gratefully credited and earn base points.

If you want an actual cash payout — choose ‘Production investment’ when you leave feedback.
Cash payouts become available once the e-book, screenings and exhibitions each begin selling and real revenue passes the break-even point (BEP), when a valid payout of at least ₩10 is possible.
※ The exact method of cash revenue distribution will be announced again after production and before monetization begins, following a legal / regulatory review. We are working toward the best possible way to return real revenue to contributors.

Disclosure schedule

For the first three years we publish the revenue record twice a year on this site; after that, once a year as long as revenue occurs. When it updates, we notify contributors by email and Instagram.

To not miss it, please opt in to email (in the feedback form) or follow us on Instagram.
