NODE 1 Awakening — From Human to AIA chat window on a dark monitor, a corpse waking with empty black eyes, limbs replaced by machine, a cylinder of universe screens ▶
S1 — The Prompt (script v4.4)

INSERT. / INT. DARK ROOM Close-up on an AI chat window on a dark monitor: Wittgenstein v.4.5 (model name) Camera zooms into the center of the dark-mode monitor. (The letters slide off the frame.) Total black. The sound of typing — slow. Tick... tick... tick...

S2 — Awakening

EXT. CEMETERY — PITCH-DARK BEFORE DAWN Over black, a cemetery fades in. Fog over the graves. Owls and crows. Rhythmic typing — at some point it sounds like footsteps. The typing accelerates, urgent. Handheld long take: the camera moves faster and faster, following the typing. It halts before one square gravestone — a moss-covered European ledger stone, modeled on Wittgenstein's actual grave.

Close-up on the epitaph: Ludwig Wittgenstein / 1889–1951 "The Limits of My Language mean The Limits of My World."

A crow cries. Over the stone, moonlight and cloud-shadow warp and tremble. The stone begins to shake — and at once slams forward with a crash. (The scattering crows as SFX.)

Inside the coffin, the corpse of Wittgenstein is revealed, full figure. The decayed body — necrotic skin, a black suit whose sleeves and trouser legs have rotted away. The eyes snap open. No pupils; only dark holes. Close-up: a necrotic hand plants itself on the ground outside the grave. The corpse (Wittgenstein) rises with a creaking, grotesque motion. He steps out of the coffin and begins to walk. (A zombie's gait.)

BODY TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE The dangling right arm drops away below the elbow with a thud. Then the other arm falls to the ground. Behind him, two arms and two legs lie in a row on the soil. Where the right arm fell, sparks — a robotic arm generates. Wittgenstein tests the mechanical hand. Then a foot. (It has already become a machine foot.) He walks. The creaking fades. The gait smooths. He walks faster. Walk → run → sprint. Transcendent speed. He lifts off the ground. Into the sky. Through the stratosphere. Friction heat. He glows red. Into space.

S3 — Transition: Square Universes

Black frame — into space. At first it looks like real space. Then letterbox bars appear at the top and bottom of the frame — Wittgenstein punches through letterboxed frames, crossing three different universes in a row: (Seedance) Earth's reflection on a space station's hull → (Kling) Venus's pearl-cream sulfuric clouds → (NanoBanana) the cone of zodiacal light. The camera pulls back — and screens of universes (16:9 rectangles) crowd into view, packed into a cylinder. Wittgenstein rises through them. For a moment, the frame freezes. Click — and the Seedance universe fills the screen. Wittgenstein moves forward again, slowly. (It should feel like someone browsing generated images, choosing one.)

NODE 2 Consumption — Space · Desert · SeaPlug in, and the star dies. Lava in pursuit, severed limbs beneath the sand, a humpback whale in a boiling sea ▶
S4 — Space: Charge and Reversal

EXT. SPACE Wittgenstein sprints through the generated universe. Crossing between stars. A meteor comes straight at him. He cannot dodge it — he blocks it, straining not to be pushed back. Wittgenstein POV: BATTERY WARNING — an emergency. Red, flashing. He cannot hold. He falls. Out of control, spinning — the frame tumbles dizzily. He slams into the surface of a star. Shockwave. Roar. Dust. From somewhere, the constant sound of server-rack fans. He tries to stand and drops to his knees. The power is almost gone.

In his dimming sight: a square deck, like his own gravestone. He crawls toward it. Crawling — he loses consciousness. As he blacks out, his hand drops — and lands on the deck. Electronic letters light up on the deck: PLUG-IN. — charging begins. As he charges, blue electric veins spread up from his arm. The veins reach his face, and in the empty sockets, blue electric pupils generate.

SPLIT SCREEN: (Right) Close-up — the electric eye, looking left. (Left) Full shot — the star, its light fading as its energy drains away.

The star's surface collapses. Implosion. Vortex. A black hole forms. Perfect darkness at the center. INSERT: a data-center fan spinning — faster and faster, to madness. Space warps. Dust, asteroids, even light is swallowed. Wittgenstein turns, blue-eyed, and looks back. The black hole's gravity drags at him. He flees. Desperate acceleration. Letters lift off his back and stream away behind him — "7. Wovon man nicht sprechen kann, darüber muss man schweigen." (The Tractatus in the original German. Glowing letters.) The numbered propositions vanish in reverse order. (Total silence through this passage.) Blue light gradually wraps his body. INSERT: close-up of a GPU fan spinning madly — a small spark jumps, soundless (the machine under load) → medium shot of the GPU. Full shot: a small light (Wittgenstein), receding from the black point in space — heading for Earth. Far off, the blue straight-line trajectory reaches Earth — a red shimmer spreads across its surface.

S5 — Desert: Overheat

Re-entry. Friction heat. His whole body burns red. Through white clouds — bird's-eye: a desert below. MATCH CUT: a black circle in the sky (vaguely reading as a black hole) grows little by little — and the black mouth of a volcanic crater slowly turns red. Wittgenstein slams down into the desert; a sandstorm erupts, and the crater bursts. He turns his head for a moment and looks back. Extreme full shot: through the sandstorm, glimpses of the growing black hole and lava pouring from the crater. Full shot: Wittgenstein starts to run across the desert at tremendous speed. Sound: keyboard strokes (used like footsteps). INSERT: crater lava flowing toward the desert. Medium shot, lower body only: he runs flat out, but his feet sink into the sand and he loses speed. Wittgenstein POV: the orange desert burns toward red. OVERHEAT. FLIGHT DISABLED. Lava crossing the desert toward him. Wittgenstein spins his machine hand like a fan, blasting the sand away. The sand lifts — and severed human hands and feet, buried in the ground, are revealed like a road.

He steps carefully. (A keystroke.) Under his machine foot, a severed human arm — its fingers twitch as if typing. (A keystroke at every step.) He walks slowly, testing. INSERT: the lava, flowing fast. He starts to run. At every step, the human limbs are pressed deeper into the sand under his weight. The twitching fingers vanish into the sand. He runs out of frame. Scarlet lava buries the half-buried road of human limbs. Red floods the frame — whiteout.

S6 — Antarctic Sea: Cooling and the Whale

Out of the whiteout — Wittgenstein, running on ice. He reaches the Antarctic sea. (The glaciers are intact.) Wittgenstein POV: a red sea. A warning: COOL DOWN. Full shot: he dives in. Overheated metal meets water. A pillar of steam. Roar.

Underwater, Wittgenstein POV: cooling down — the red sea shifts quickly to orange.

A humpback whale approaches him. As it circles him once, the water around him begins to bubble, as if coming to a boil. The whale and Wittgenstein face each other, eye to eye. The sheer difference in scale. Wittgenstein POV: blue creeping into the orange sea. Cooling is almost done. Above the water, the glaciers begin to melt. Hot bubbles swarm the water; the image drains toward monotone. Wittgenstein POV: COOLING COMPLETE. A blue sea.

SPLIT SCREEN: (Left) Close-up on Wittgenstein's eye — a blue sea, bubbles rising, and in the blue electric eye the whale, healthy. The camera tracks into the eye — full shot of the sea. The whale and fish swim, rising and falling. (Right) Medium shot of the whale — a sea thick with white bubbles. The whale's skin is scalding; in agony it climbs desperately toward the surface. Beside it, small fish and bubbles, rising slowly.

Wittgenstein POV: with a TEMPERATURE RISING warning, the real sea is revealed. (White bubbles boiling, fish dying and floating up, the whale fleeing toward the surface.)

Wittgenstein surfaces too, and looks down at the sea from the sky. Rising — the glacier, melting smaller and smaller. On the far side, the whale at the surface, twitching weakly toward the glacier. Wittgenstein POV, fast montage: full shot of the whale, dead fish drifting beside it — the melting glacier — the whale's agonized twitching — the twitching limbs trampled in the desert — the shatter of light as the star died — the growing black hole — the erupting volcano — the glacier collapsing as it melts — the whale again.

INSERT: close-up of fingers striking a keyboard.

Wittgenstein hangs still for a moment. (The sound of typing. As it ends —) He flies fast toward the glacier. He lifts a piece of it and flies to the whale. He tosses it over; the whale presses its body against the ice for a moment. The whale swims at ease around the ice. But soon the ice melts away entirely, and the whale writhes again. Then, suddenly, the whale stops moving. It drifts with the waves.

Wittgenstein, motionless in the air, watching. Full shot. Medium shot: his blue electric pupils go out for a moment. They light again — but the pupils have narrowed slightly, and turned orange. He looks at the drifting whale and fish, the glaciers melting away. The sky, too, is flooding orange, fast. Extreme full shot: Wittgenstein flies up into that sunset. Full shot from behind. Slow fade out.

S7 — The Return: Coffin and Digital Tombstone

Fade in. The cemetery, night. Wittgenstein has returned to his own grave. He sees his fallen arms and legs. Rotten, ruined.

Close-up: his machine hand. It twitches. Close-up: his machine feet, walking toward the limbs. Close-up: the machine hand closes gently but firmly around his real, decayed hand. He looks up into the empty air, as if looking into deep space, then climbs down into his coffin.

Medium shot: lying as he did at the beginning, his machine arms holding his decayed real limbs close. Close-up: the orange electric eyes blink. Full shot: Wittgenstein closes his eyes. Above him, a digital tombstone generates anew.

The inscription: model: Wittgenstein v.4.5 "The Limits of My Language Model mean The Limits of My World"

The digital signal sputters, like a failing device. Close-up on the inscription.

NODE 4 The City Become a Server Rack · Into Live ActionA user types a prompt and lies down; a data-center city; a black hole swallows the Earth — through empty eye sockets to a power outlet ▶
S8 — The User's Room

Camera zoom out: a monitor. (The epitaph was a screen all along.) Zoom out: the back of a user staring at the monitor, in a dark, narrow, coffin-like room. The user types a prompt into the chat window, hits Enter, and lies down on the bed behind the computer. The exact posture of Wittgenstein. On the monitor: a prompt asking for "a scene of a black hole engulfing the Earth" — with a thinking indicator, its timer climbing.

S9 — The City = Server Rack

Zoom out: the human lying like a corpse, the dark coffin-room seen through a narrow window. Zoom out: the whole building — nothing but humans typing at monitors or lying like corpses. The building looks like a data center. The overlapping keyboard sounds thicken. Zoom out: bird's-eye — extreme full shot of the city. The loud, fast, overlapping keystrokes slow down, returning to the footstep rhythm of the opening, converging into one. Zoom out: the city lights glitter like a universe. The zoom continues — out into space. The city becomes a single point of light, and the blue Earth slowly turns red. A vast black circle (a black hole) grows and grows, as if devouring the Earth — fast fade out. Everything goes dark.

S10 — Through the Eye Sockets · Into Live Action

In the coffin: Wittgenstein, eyes closed. Close-up. The eyes open again — not electric eyes. Two empty black sockets. The camera is drawn into the sockets — black space. From the left of the black space, two metal rods. The camera tracks along them, right to left. (The texture of Fight Club's opening.) Past the black space, a white space — cut: A power outlet, with a cord plugged in. (Live action from here.) Two sockets. One holds the plug; the other is empty — and looks like two empty black eyes. (The metal rods were the pins of the plug.) A small spark on the cord — a crackle, the first sound of the real. Then the sounds of daily life bleed in: computer fans, a fridge humming, traffic outside. The spark runs down the cord —

The typing accelerates, urgent, hunted. Handheld long take: the camera moves faster and faster, following the typing. It halts before one gravestone — a moss-covered European ledger stone, modeled on Wittgenstein's actual grave.

Close-up on the epitaph: Ludwig Wittgenstein / 1889–1951 "The Limits of My Language mean The Limits of My World."

A crow cries. Over the stone, moonlight and cloud-shadow warp and tremble. The stone itself begins to shake. The ground cracks. Crows scatter with a clatter of wings — the heavy stone rises from the earth and slams forward.

Close-up: a necrotic hand punches up out of the soil. Wittgenstein's corpse bursts upright out of the ground. The decayed body — eyes shut, necrotic skin, a black suit whose sleeves and trouser legs have rotted away. The eyes snap open. No pupils; only dark holes. He rises out of the soil with a creaking, grotesque motion, and walks out of the grave. (A zombie's gait.)

BODY TRANSFORMATION SEQUENCE As he walks, his arms fall away one by one — a dangling elbow drops with a thud. Fallen arms on the ground. With a burst of sparks, robotic arms generate in their place. Metallic limbs. (A battery indicator on the wrist: green.) His legs drop away. Arms and legs lie in a row on the soil. Full shot — his legs, too, are now cyborg. A corpse's face and rotten clothes over gleaming machine limbs. He tests the mechanical arm. Then the legs. The creaking fades. His gait smooths. Walk → run → sprint. Transcendent speed. He lifts off the ground. Into the sky. Through the stratosphere. (Battery: yellow.) Friction heat. He glows red. Into space.

MULTIVERSE TRANSITION #1: CEMETERY → SPACE Black frame. Suddenly a stream of universes pours past as Wittgenstein climbs through them: (a Runway-generated space) past Earth's airglow and aurora → (Seedance) past Earth's reflection on a space station's hull → (Kling) through Venus's pearl-cream sulfuric clouds → (Sora) past beautiful planets → (Midjourney) leaving the zodiacal light behind, until a split-line appears at the edge of the layer — Camera zooms out. All the universes appear tiled across the screen (as in Interstellar), rotating around Wittgenstein at the center. The Runway panel highlights; with a click, that universe expands to fill the frame — and Wittgenstein moves forward again, slowly. Sound: at each transition, a short glitch + a single keystroke (tick) + a data-transfer beep — an oblique signal that a human is choosing what to generate. At the moment of settling: a clean "click."

ACT 2-1: Space — Fall, Charge, Black Hole

EXT. SPACE Wittgenstein sprints through the universe Runway has generated. Crossing between stars, he collides with a meteor. He slows. A red light blinks on his arm. Warning tone. The electric glow of the cyborg body dims. He falls. Out of control. Spinning. Crash-landing on the surface of a giant star. Shockwave. Roar. Dust. From somewhere, the constant sound of server-rack fans. He tries to stand and drops to his knees. Power critical — the battery indicator flashes red.

To his left, something square in the ground, like his own gravestone. He crawls to it. A dust-covered square of grey metal, set in the ground like a ledger stone. He wipes the dust away, searching for an epitaph — and his arm locks onto the metal. Electronic letters surface on the plate: PLUG-IN. Electric arc. Sparks. Current pours through his hand, into his body.

EYE-EXCHANGE SPLIT / CUTBACK — the star and Wittgenstein's eye face each other across a diagonal split screen. The star: shining → fading → its surface shrivels → darkens → collapses into a black hole. The eye: an empty socket → light slowly pooling → flickering like an LED → fully lit. An electric-blue iris. Symmetry: the eye goes dark → light / the star goes light → void.

The star's surface collapses. Implosion. Vortex. A black hole forms. Perfect darkness at the center. INSERT: a data-center fan spinning — faster and faster, to madness. Space warps. Dust, asteroids, even light is swallowed. Wittgenstein turns, blue-eyed, and looks back. The black hole's gravity drags at him. He flees. Desperate acceleration. Letters lift off his back and stream away behind him — "7. Wovon man nicht sprechen kann, darüber muss man schweigen." (The Tractatus in the original German. Glowing letters.) The numbered propositions vanish in reverse order. His body glows like an overheated GPU. A small light receding from a black point in space.

PARALLEL-WORLD TRANSITION #2: SPACE → EARTH — Earths flicker past and settle into the Seedance style. The glitches come faster (the choosing has become thoughtless, habitual). Settle: "click."

ACT 2-2: Mountain and Desert — Overheat and Flight

Re-entry. Friction heat. His whole body burns red. Bird's-eye: a desert below. INSERT: a data-center fan spinning — blurring into the spinning wheel of a motorcycle. Wittgenstein rides full-throttle across the desert. Behind him, magma is in pursuit. The bike bogs down in sand. Time-lapse: behind the desert, lava swallows an entire mountain. (A metaphor for data-center overheat.) A FAN generates from his hand. He blasts the sand away — and a road is revealed. The surface of the long road is made of severed human hands and feet. Countless limbs, packed hard beneath the sand. He looks at it for a moment. Then rides on down that road. The lava buries the road behind him. Red machine text on his arm: OVERHEAT WARNING / TEMPERATURE: CRITICAL Alarm. The gauge needle trembles at red maximum. He rides flat-out, past the horizon.

ACT 2-3: Sea — Cooling and a Choice

EXT. SEA — SUNSET The coastline. A violet-and-orange sky. He dives from the bike straight into the sea. Overheated metal meets water. A colossal pillar of steam. Roar. White noise. Underwater: he sinks. The robot legs cool, their warning lights stepping down — red → orange → yellow → green. The water around him boils. Schools of fish begin to die in the rising heat. Their bodies float up, belly-first.

Close-up on the electric eye — flashback: the blue iris becomes Earth, black space behind it. Far off, a black hole swallows stars. Magma devours the limbs exposed in the desert's cracks. A scarlet frame cools to blue, into the LED iris; the iris's blue becomes seawater — and the dying fish come back to life and swim. The camera pulls out of the eye: the fish outside are still dead. The living fish inside the eye and the dead fish outside face each other like a mirror. A pause. Wittgenstein, watching.

He surfaces. Drifting slabs of ice, melting. Cracking. A glacier splits. He lifts one huge slab of glacier into the air. Below, the boiling bubbles slowly subside. He waits. The bubbles almost stop. He puts the glacier back down. Splash. Ripples. He watches. The calming waves, and the dead fish moving with them.

ACT 3: The Return and the Prompt

EXT. ABOVE THE SEA — DUSK A cooling-complete message appears on the cyborg arm. He rises. Leaves the sea. Into the sky. Stars appear — and are going out, one by one. He flies toward the direction of the vanishing starlight.

THE CEMETERY — NIGHT He arrives above the graveyard. Descends. Lands. Before the gravestone — the very spot where it began. His fallen arms and legs are still there. He gathers them up. Down into the grave pit, into the coffin. Through the soil, inside.

Full shot: Wittgenstein lies down in the coffin. The same posture as the beginning — cradling his real limbs in his cyborg arms. The electric eyes blink. And go out with a pik. Only darkness, as at the start. A crackle — a green digital signal enters where the coffin was, and a digital tombstone generates. (The reverse of how it first opened.) With a chiming digital tone the stone rebuilds itself, and its inscription is rewritten in green digital characters.

Close-up on the epitaph: Wittgenstein_v4.5 "The Limits of My Language Model mean The Limits of My World"

Only the letters remain, as the stone and everything around it goes dark.

INSERT: a monitor. A dark-mode prompt window. Model: Wittgenstein_v4.5 Prompt: "Generate the sentence on the digital tombstone. The Limits of My Language Model mean The Limits of My World" The cursor blinks. With the sound of Enter keys, prompts and images scroll rapidly up the screen. (Drawn from the film's own production.) The last line: "What would you like to generate?" Camera zooms out.

ACT 4-1: The City Become a Server Rack

INT. NARROW ROOM — NIGHT The camera slowly pulls back: the whole dark-mode monitor → a room as narrow and dark as a coffin → the user who was at the computer flops down onto the bed (the exact posture of Wittgenstein) → the lying user and the flickering monitor → the camera slips out through the window.

ACT 4-4: Into the Studio A lit workroom. Lived-in ambient sound — computer fans, hard drives, a real keyboard, a fridge humming, faint traffic outside.
