Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and when we delete it

This site publishes its revenue and how that revenue is shared. For the same reason, we publish how we handle your information.

Effective 10 September 2026 · Last revised 10 September 2026

0. Logging in creates an account

There is no separate sign-up screen, but logging in is signing up

This site has no username-and-password registration page. The moment you log in with Google or an email link, an account is created, and from then on it accumulates:

  • your display name and login identifier
  • the feedback and guestbook entries you leave, and whether they were adopted
  • your accrued points and revenue shares
  • your choice of how you want to be credited

So logging in is not merely authentication — it is registration. To pay you, we have to keep remembering who contributed what. If you would rather not, the guestbook offers “write without login” — no account is created, and no points accrue.

4. What happens to your rewards if you leave

Set out separately because it is the question most likely to matter

Closing your account does not erase rewards already earned. A share in adopted work is something we owe you, and deleting an account does not cancel a debt. What it can do is leave us with no way to pay you — so when you close your account you choose one of three options.

OptionRewardWhat we keep
A. Settle now Take what has accrued so far; share ends Settlement record for 5 years (statutory); everything else destroyed
B. Keep your share Continue to be paid at each revenue report Only what payment requires — name, contact, contribution and share record. Everything else destroyed
C. Waive it No further reward Everything destroyed except statutory records

Credit works differently.

  • It cannot be recalled from editions already published or distributed (e-book, screening prints, site archive). What has gone out cannot be pulled back.
  • On request we will change or remove it in editions produced afterwards.
  • You may equally ask us to keep your name. The fact of your contribution does not disappear, and the right to have it acknowledged belongs to you.

※ If you choose B and we cannot reach you for three years, we destroy what we held and move the unpaid amount to the donation line on the revenue page. That transfer is itself recorded in the revenue report.

To close your account, write to cinmaite0@gmail.com.

6. Your rights

  • Access — we will tell you what we hold about you
  • Correction — we will fix anything inaccurate
  • Deletion — you may request it (subject to the reward exceptions in section 4)
  • Restriction — you may ask us to stop processing
  • Withdrawal — email consent can be revoked at any time

Write to cinmaite0@gmail.com and we will act within 10 days and tell you the outcome.

※ Children under 14 may not use the site without the consent of a legal guardian. If we find such data, we destroy it.
